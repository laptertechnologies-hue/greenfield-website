Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Greenfield Secondary School Masindi ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Greenfield Secondary School Masindi mobile application and official website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from users of our mobile application. The app is designed to provide information about school programs, curriculum, admissions, fees, and to allow access to student and parent school portals. Any portal logins are processed securely by our external school system server.

2. Log Data

Like many app operators, we may collect information that your device sends whenever you visit our Service ("Log Data"). This Log Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device type, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

3. Cookies

Cookies are files with small amount of data, which may include an anonymous unique identifier. Our website utilizes standard cookies to enhance user experience. The mobile application does not store cookies directly on your device, except for temporary session cookies utilized to maintain secure logins within web portal views.

4. Security

The security of your personal information is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect any login credentials, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
